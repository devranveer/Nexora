Nexora 🚀

AI-Powered Opportunity Discovery & Eligibility Platform

Nexora is an AI-powered platform designed to help students discover relevant opportunities, scholarships, internships, and programs from online sources. Instead of manually searching across multiple websites, students can use Nexora to extract useful information, evaluate eligibility, and organize opportunities in one place.

💡 The Problem

Students often miss valuable opportunities because information is scattered across different websites, eligibility criteria are difficult to understand, and application deadlines are easy to forget.

✨ Our Solution

Nexora aims to simplify opportunity discovery through AI-assisted information extraction, eligibility matching, and deadline management.

🚀 Key Features

🔎 Opportunity Extraction: Extract structured opportunity information from text and supported website URLs.

🎓 Scholarship Matching: Help students identify scholarships that match their eligibility criteria.

🎯 Eligibility Matching: Compare student profiles with available opportunities.

📅 Opportunity Calendar: Organize important dates and application deadlines.

🌐 URL-Based Data Collection: Submit a webpage URL to retrieve and process relevant information.

🤖 AI-Powered Processing: Convert unstructured opportunity information into a more organized format.

📊 Opportunity Dashboard: Access discovered opportunities through a centralized interface.

Note: Features described here represent the intended functionality. Some capabilities may still be under development.
